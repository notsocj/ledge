# Mac newsletters and sites

Short, personal pitches do best. One email per outlet, sent to the tips or contact address on their
own site (look it up when you send; don't guess). Send it a few days before launch with a free license,
and follow up once a week later if there's no reply.

## Where to pitch

| Outlet | Why it fits | How to reach them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MacStories | Covers indie Mac apps in depth, newsletter included | Contact or tips page on macstories.net |
| 9to5Mac | Regular roundups of new Mac apps | Tips address on 9to5mac.com |
| MacRumors | Covers notable new Mac utilities | Tips form on macrumors.com |
| The Sweet Setup | Picks of the best Mac apps | Contact page on thesweetsetup.com |
| Mac Power Users (Relay) | Listeners love workflow utilities | Feedback form on relay.fm |
| Indie dev newsletters on Substack/Buttondown | Small but they reply | Search "indie Mac apps newsletter" |
| Claude Code / AI dev newsletters | For the agent alerts and usage limits | Their submission forms |

For the AI-dev outlets, lead with the developer angle (agent alerts and limits), not with music.

## Pitch email

**Subject:** Ledge: the MacBook notch, set up around what you do

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name], and I make Ledge, a Mac app that turns the space around the notch into the tools
> you use all day. It launches on [date].
>
> Hover the notch and it opens into a small island: what's playing with synced lyrics, your week, a
> file shelf, clipboard history. When it's closed, short alerts slide out beside it, like volume, a
> meeting about to start, or Claude Code waiting on you.
>
> The new part: on first launch you pick what you do (developer, designer, student, and so on) and it
> sets itself up around that. Developers get Claude and Codex limits, agent alerts and their
> localhost ports; designers get a color picker and screenshots on the shelf.
>
> It's native Swift, keeps everything on the Mac, and costs $19.99 once.
>
> Here's a license so you can try it: [license]
> Video (40 seconds): [link]
> Images and icon: [press kit link]
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [your name]

## Press kit (one zip, linked in the pitch)

- `icon.png` at 1024×1024 (export from `Branding/AppIcon.svg`)
- Five stills from `site/assets`: `hero-open`, `setup-personas`, `home-developer`, `tab-stats`, and
  one banner, each on the dusk background
- `demo.mp4`
- A text file with the one-paragraph description, price, requirements, and the site link
