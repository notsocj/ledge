## Notifications that stay useful

- Important alerts take priority, repeated alerts are grouped, and pending alerts expire.
- Fresh alerts wait while Home is open. Immediate volume, microphone and color feedback stays responsive.
- Use **Preview notification** from Ledge's menu or Settings to see the island's jelly animation.
- Turn notification sounds on or off in Settings.

## Easier updates

Ledge now checks for updates in the background. Use **Check for Updates…** in its menu for a manual check,
or change automatic checks in **Settings → Updates**. You choose when to install and relaunch.

Updates and the update feed are signed. Your notes, calendar and clipboard are not sent with update checks.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon.
